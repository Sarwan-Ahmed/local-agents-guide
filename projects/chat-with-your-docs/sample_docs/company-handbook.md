# Aurora Robotics — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

This is a fictional handbook used only to demonstrate retrieval — swap this folder for your own notes, docs, or codebase once the pipeline works.

## Vacation policy

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per year, credited monthly. Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

## Remote work policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval. Fully remote arrangements require sign-off from your manager and must be renewed every 6 months.

## Expense reimbursement

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports submitted after 30 days require director approval and may be denied.
