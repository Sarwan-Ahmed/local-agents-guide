# Aurora Home Hub — Product FAQ (excerpt)

Fictional product documentation, used only to demonstrate retrieval.

## Battery life

The Aurora Home Hub's backup battery lasts approximately 14 hours during a power outage, and takes about 4 hours to fully recharge once power is restored.

## Warranty

The Home Hub ships with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Water damage and drops are not covered.

## Wi-Fi requirements

The Home Hub requires a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network for initial setup. Once connected, it can use either 2.4GHz or 5GHz for normal operation.
